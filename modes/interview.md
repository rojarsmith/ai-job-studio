# Mode: interview (S8 prep)

For each `<workspace>/applications/NNN/interview.md` whose `## Prep` section is still the placeholder, build a preparation pack. Text files only. Workspace path: `node scripts/workspace.mjs path`.

Inputs: the report `reports/NNN-*.md` (job text, score lines, knock-outs, gaps), `cv.md`, `applications/NNN/cv.md`, `cover.md`, `review.md`.

Write under `## Prep`:

1. **Role in one paragraph** — what they actually need, inferred from the posting, and the two or three things the interviewer will probe.
2. **Likely questions, 12 to 15**, grouped: role-specific technical / leadership, behavioural, the gaps from `review.md` (they will ask about these), knock-outs (visa, salary, notice, relocation). For each: the question, a 3 to 5 line answer outline built only from `cv.md` evidence, and the proof point to cite.
3. **Stories to have ready, 4 to 6** — STAR outlines from `cv.md` achievements with numbers.
4. **Questions to ask them, 5** — specific to this team and posting.
5. **Red flags to listen for** — from the report's red_flags and the company meta.

Rules: no invented facts; where `cv.md` is thin, say so and suggest what the human should prepare. Keep the whole pack under two pages of text.
